---
name: project-decision
description: Preserve an explicit architectural or product decision with rationale, alternatives and consequences in Project Memory.
---

Identify the project and obtain its evidence bundle. Record only a decision actually made by the user or authorized work. Call project_record_decision with title, decision, rationale, alternatives, consequences and scoped evidenceRefs. Preserve unresolved alternatives as unresolved; do not invent approval or turn a suggestion into an adopted decision.
